- Use, study, modify and share the code under the terms of the GPL-3.0.
- Build KoMD from source for your own use, unchanged, under its original name.
- Refer to KoMD by name to describe it truthfully, e.g. "a fork of KoMD" or "compatible with KoMD".

## What requires permission

If you **distribute** a modified version (a fork), or a build that is not an official release, you must:

1. **Change the name** to one that cannot be confused with KoMD.
2. **Replace the icon and logo** with your own artwork.
3. Change the application identifiers so your build does not replace or impersonate the official
   app (e.g. `bundleID` on macOS, `applicationId` on Android, the MSIX identity on Windows).
4. Not suggest that your version is official or endorsed by the KoMD author.

You may state that your project is "based on KoMD" with a link to the original repository.

Any other use of the name or logo (merchandise, app store listings, domains, commercial
services) needs prior written permission.
